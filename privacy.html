Website privacy and cookies

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This page covers the publisher website at bemintalitet.github.io and the THE WHATEVER GAME website under /TheWhateverGame/. The publisher is Benjamin Foss Kristoffersen / UniqaApps. Contact TheWhateverGame@uniqapps.space about website privacy.

Website AdSense advertising is currently paused. The publisher and game landing pages do not load the AdSense advertising script. Ownership verification metadata and the public ads.txt file do not themselves request ads. Android advertising is separate and is described in the app privacy policy.

Hosting and ordinary page requests

GitHub Pages hosts this website. When you visit, your browser sends information such as your IP address, requested URL and browser information to the hosting provider to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement for its processing and retention practices. We do not operate a separate website analytics service or require a website account.

Browser game storage

The browser game saves progress and preferences in your browser's local storage. Depending on the game version, this includes scores, achievements, unlocked items and audio settings. The site may also use a service worker cache to load game files. Clearing site data removes locally saved progress and cached files; private browsing and a different browser may use separate storage. Keep this in mind before clearing your browser data.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

If website advertising is enabled in a future update, Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. Ad delivery may process IP addresses, browser and device information, identifiers and ad interactions for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Before personalized website advertising is enabled for visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, a Google-certified consent management platform must be configured and tested. Applicable consent choices will be presented on the website, with a way to revisit them. The Android app's consent form does not provide consent for this website.

You can manage Google's personalized advertising through Google's ad settings and learn about third-party advertising choices at YourAdChoices. Read how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. These links do not activate advertising on this website.

Links and contact messages

Links to Google Play, GitHub and other platforms take you to services with their own privacy policies. If you email us, your message and address are processed to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment card details or other unnecessary sensitive information. Contact us if you want a support message deleted, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Your choices and updates

You can control cookies and site storage in your browser. Requests concerning data processed by the hosting or advertising provider can also be directed to that provider. Contact us for help with access, correction or deletion requests concerning information you have sent us. Where applicable, you may complain to your data protection authority. This page will be updated when website data practices change.
