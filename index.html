About THE WHATEVER GAME

THE WHATEVER GAME is an independent arcade collection for Android and the web. A run can move between platforming, rhythm, maze, survival, puzzle and boss encounters without asking the player to choose a single genre.

  • 31 gameplay genres, four bosses and multiple changing worlds.
  • Daily challenges, achievements, unlockable skins and local progress.
  • Pocket RPG as an additional game mode.
  • No required account and no purchase required to play.

Advertising and player choice

Website advertising is currently paused while our AdSense application and consent setup are reviewed. The website and Android app use separate advertising integrations. Read our website privacy and cookie information for details about hosting, browser storage and future advertising choices.

The Android edition is supported by carefully limited advertising. Small banner ads are reserved for menu screens, full-screen ads are only considered after completed runs, and optional rewarded ads let the player choose whether to watch an ad for an in-game benefit. Ads do not cover active gameplay.

Players in applicable regions receive Google's privacy choices before advertising is requested. Details about data used by advertising providers and how to contact the publisher are available in the privacy policy.

Support and frequently asked questions

Where can I play?

The supported Android release is available on Google Play. A browser edition is also available through the game website.

Is an account required?

No. Core progress is stored locally on the device, and the game can be started without registering an account.

How do I report a problem?

Include the device model, Android version and a short description when writing to TheWhateverGame@uniqapps.space.
